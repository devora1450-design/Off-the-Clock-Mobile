Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

Retire ("we", "the app") is a retirement countdown application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Birthday and gender (to calculate retirement date)
  • Country selection (for retirement age and holiday calculations)
  • Personal preferences (vacation days, religion, dreams)
  • Calendar check-off history

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data for ad personalization as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Usage data

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The app uses:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Expo (app framework)

Data Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time using the "Start Fresh" button in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
