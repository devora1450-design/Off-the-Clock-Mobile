## App Name
Retire - Retirement Countdown

## Short Description (80 chars)
Count down to retirement day by day. Track work days, holidays & savings.

## Full Description
Retire is your daily motivation to keep going. Watch your remaining work days tick down, track holidays, and plan your path to financial freedom.

FEATURES:
- Precise countdown: See exactly how many calendar days and work days remain until retirement
- Smart holiday tracking: Automatically calculates public holidays and religious holidays for 10 countries
- Interactive calendar: Tap to check off each day as it passes
- $1 Million calculator: See when you'll hit your savings goal at 7% annual compound return
- Career progress bar: Visualize how far you've come
- Dreams board: Pick what you're counting down to
- Birthday celebration: A special surprise on your birthday
- Country-aware: Retirement ages, weekends, and holidays for US, UK, Canada, Israel, Germany, France, Japan, Australia, Brazil, India

PERFECT FOR:
- Anyone counting down to retirement
- People who want daily motivation that the end is in sight
- Savers tracking their path to financial independence

All your data stays on your device. No account required.
